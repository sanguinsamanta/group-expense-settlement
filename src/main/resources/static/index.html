💡 Greedy Settlement Algorithm Architecture

The backend implements a deterministic Greedy Matching Algorithm to minimize debt transfers:

  • Step 1: Compute each member's net balance: netBalance = totalPaid - assignedShare.
  • Step 2: Separate debtors (negative balance) and creditors (positive balance).
  • Step 3: Sort debtors and creditors descending by amounts in $O(N \log N)$.
  • Step 4: Match the largest debtor with the largest creditor, transferring $\min(\text{debtorAmount}, \text{creditorAmount})$.
  • Step 5: Repeat until all balances reach ₹0.00.
  • Note: While minimizing cash transactions globally is NP-complete (reducible from the Subset Sum Problem), this greedy approach guarantees full settlement with zero cash drift in $O(N \log N)$.
